Academics & Enrichment

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-Kindergarten program is designed for children aged 3-4 years and focuses on play-based learning, socialization, and early literacy skills.

📚

Elementary

Our Elementary program serves students from Kindergarten to Grade 5 and emphasizes foundational skills in reading, writing, mathematics, and science.

📚

Middle School

Our Middle School program serves students from Grade 6 to 8 and focuses on academic rigor, critical thinking, and character development.

💻

STEM

Our STEM program is designed to foster a love of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics through hands-on learning, coding, and robotics.

🎨

Arts

Our Arts program offers a range of creative activities, including music, dance, visual arts, and drama, to help students express themselves and develop their talents.

🏋️‍♀️

Athletics

Our Athletics program encourages physical activity, teamwork, and sportsmanship through a range of intra-school and inter-school competitions.
