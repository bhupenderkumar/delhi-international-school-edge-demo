Our Mission & History

Delhi International School Edge was founded in 2010 with a vision to provide a high-quality education that prepares students for success in an increasingly complex and interconnected world. Our school is committed to academic excellence, character development, and community engagement.

Our teaching approach is designed to be student-centered, inquiry-based, and inclusive. We believe in fostering a love of learning and encouraging students to explore their interests, passions, and strengths.

At Delhi International School Edge, we offer a wide range of extracurricular activities and programs that promote physical, emotional, and social growth. Our students have the opportunity to participate in sports, music, art, and community service, among other activities.
