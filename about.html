Our Mission & History

Established in 2003, Delhi International School Edge has a rich history of academic excellence and community service. Our school has grown from a small group of students to a thriving community of over 200 learners.

Our teaching approach is centered around a child-centered learning philosophy, which emphasizes critical thinking, creativity, and collaboration. Our experienced faculty uses various pedagogical tools and strategies to make learning engaging and fun.

At Delhi International School Edge, we believe that extracurricular activities play a vital role in shaping our students' character and personality. We offer a range of sports, music, art, and drama programs that cater to diverse interests and talents.
